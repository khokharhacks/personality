Bio & Mission

Dr. Umar Khokhar is an Associate Professor, seasoned cybersecurity researcher, and dedicated open educator. With a deep passion for democratizing higher education in Information Technology, he has authored comprehensive open-access textbooks designed for university students and industry professionals alike.

Beyond academia, Dr. Khokhar conducts high-stakes penetration testing, security audits, and specialized technical keynotes. He actively mentors aspiring security analysts, software engineers, and post-graduate researchers worldwide.
